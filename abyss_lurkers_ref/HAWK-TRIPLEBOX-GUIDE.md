# Throwaway Hawk triplebox guide — T4/T5/T6 Dark Abyssals

Community guide (Abyssal Lurkers), saved 2026-09-02. Directly relevant to the 3x Hawk
spider-tank FleetBrain scenario — the tactics below (aggro split on the lead ADCU hawk,
burn-away kiting, per-spawn target priority) are the reference behaviour the fleet bot
should reproduce.

---

Here is the classic throwaway hawk fit, for doing t4/t5/t6 Dark Abyssals. This fit is
meant to be tripleboxed (aka using 3 characters or you and 2 other friends) and is meant
to be used to earn back the entire comp after one or two runs allowing for easy profit to
be made despite how easy it is to die in the comp.

## What tier should I run?

- **T4**: Very easy, can be easily done with newly injected toons, great place to learn on
  how to fly hawks
- **T5**: Middle of the road, can be done with level 4 skills and can easily accommodate
  more than 1 person without compromising any potential runs. Only real threat are angels
- **T6**: Very hard to fly with more than 1 person, requires MAX missile skills to not
  time out, very easy to die

## Basic room walkthrough

When you enter, have your lead hawk with a fitted ADCU manual pilot away from the spawn to
draw aggro while simultaneously activating AB and one shield booster on all hawks, then
regrouping the two other hawks in your fleet comp. You then run away from the enemy spawn
while killing any threats until the spawn is dead. After the entire spawn is dead,
approach the can and pop it with the railgun and proceed into the next room (you may elect
to approach the cache with enemies left if you are confident they cannot kill you).

## What do I focus fire on

- Battleships
- Battlecruisers (Drekavac and Rogue Drone BC)
- Anything Trig (Damaviks, Kikimoras, Vedmaks, etc)
- Angel Cruisers (Lucifer Cynabal, Elite Lucifer Cynabal)
- Drifter/Sleeper/Sansha Cruisers

## What do I free fire on

- Everything else

## Dangerous spawns and a general guide on how to do them

- **Angels**: Death room, burn away and heat both shield boosters on the tank hawk, ADC
  when your shields get low. Target priority is Cynabal -> Elite Lucifer Cynabal ->
  Lucifer Fury -> Else
- **Vedmaks**: Hard room due to potentially copious amounts of neuts and webs. Kill
  starving/tangling damaviks to get rid of annoying EWAR before killing Starving Vedmaks
  -> Harrowing Vedmaks. If there are too many tangling damaviks, kill neuting
  Damaviks/Vedmaks instead
- **Rogue Drone Frigates**: Burn in the opposite direction and kill all lance frigates, do
  not shoot the snarecasters they are very hard to kill and they'll catch up to you
  anyways with or without webs.
- **EDENCOM**: Burn around, avoid blue clouds. Biggest threat are Pacifiers then Marshals,
  after that it doesn't really matter what you kill.
- **Overmind**: Hard because of time, kill the escorting frigates, then orbit at 500 with
  2 hawks and manually orbit to keep transversal up with the one getting aggro'd.

## Tips and tricks

- It's important to have an open mentality about this, you are gonna die, that's what you
  get for choosing the 150m hawk fleet instead of the 1b or 3b fleets. There will be
  spawns that aren't runnable. If you feel frustrated, try doing something else as you
  being tilted may let you make critical mistakes that can make you even angrier
- Be very wary of burnout
- What's the survival rate against angels? T4 80%, T5 50%, T6 1%
- Haul in multiple hawks sets before you try this with the right ammo drugs and boosters,
  you will die a lot when you first start out. Also set your home station to the system
  you run them in for ease
- Use drugs only on t6s (blue pill, pyro, crash); you may elect to use blue pill and
  hardshell in t5s and t4s when facing angels.

## I'm sick of dying all the time, how do I save my precious killboard

There is a more expensive fleet (referenced video in the original post) that can survive
angels 95% of the time but costs a lot more money. You should probably only do this if you
are confident in your abilities to run cheap hawks, as they allow you to make mistakes
critical to learning how to master dark hawks. They fly literally the same as the
throwaways but with a lot more leeway for harder spawns.
